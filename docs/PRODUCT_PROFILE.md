# Eagle Bot — Official Product Profile

## Identity
Eagle Bot is an independent governed AI work and coordination platform.

Telegram is a communication channel, not the product.

## Mission
> Make people and organizations dramatically more capable by giving them trustworthy AI Coworkers that can understand goals, do meaningful work, and remain accountable to humans.

## Differentiation
- Outcome-first work
- Persistent context and memory
- Specialized AI Coworkers
- Human-controlled autonomy
- Permission-based actions
- Evidence and uncertainty awareness
- Cross-channel identity
- Auditable decisions and actions
- Real-world information interfaces
- Continuous evaluation and improvement

## Initial Coworkers
Generalist, Researcher, Builder, Analyst, Operator and Educator.

These are role definitions. They are not claims that all listed capabilities are already implemented.

## Governance
More capability → more responsibility → stronger governance.

Low-risk information may be handled directly. High-impact actions require appropriate authorization. Critical actions remain under meaningful human control.

## Engineering Law
Observe → Learn → Verify → Evaluate → Upgrade → Monitor → Correct.

> Learn from the world. Never blindly copy it.

## Truthfulness Boundary
Eagle must never claim intelligence, world awareness, autonomy or tool capability that has not been implemented and tested.
